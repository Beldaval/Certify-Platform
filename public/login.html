Certsift

What Certsift does. Certsift lets you generate, personalize, and deliver professional certificates for training programs, seminars, church activities, and similar events. You choose a template, fill in recipient details, and we generate a PDF, email it to your recipients, and keep a record that lets anyone verify the certificate's authenticity later.

Your account and what we collect. To use Certsift, we collect your name, organization name, email address, and — if you provide it — a phone number, so we can identify your account and reach you if needed. We also keep a record of your token balance, payment history, and the batches and certificates you generate. We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we only share what's necessary to process payments (via Paystack) and send emails (via our email provider).

Certificate files and how long we keep things. Your downloadable copy remains available for 21 days after generation, after which the file itself is removed from our storage. However, the verification record for every certificate — recipient's name, program title, issuing organization, issue date, and certificate number — is kept permanently, so anyone can verify a certificate's authenticity through our public verification page, even years later.

Tokens and payments. Tokens are a prepaid credit (1 certificate = 50 tokens). New accounts receive a free trial grant. Purchased tokens are non-refundable except where a certificate genuinely fails to generate, in which case those tokens are automatically returned. We do not store your card details; all payment processing is handled directly by Paystack.

What we expect from you. You're responsible for the accuracy of information you enter. Certsift is a tool for legitimate certificates for real programs and achievements; you agree not to use it to create fraudulent, misleading, or impersonating documents. You're responsible for keeping your password secure.

No warranty, and a word on liability. We work hard to keep Certsift running reliably, but can't promise perfect uptime. Certsift isn't liable for indirect losses, and our total liability to you is limited to the amount you've paid us in the past 2 months in tokens.

Changes to this policy. If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the version number and date, and — where significant — ask existing users to re-accept it.

Contact. Questions about this policy, your data, or your account: +234-701-3527-505.
