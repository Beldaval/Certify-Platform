Certsift — Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3rd September, 2026 · Version 1.0

Welcome to Certsift. By creating an account, you agree to the terms below. We've tried to keep this readable rather than burying it in legal jargon — if anything here is unclear, reach out to us at +234-701-3527-505.

What Certsift does

Certsift lets you generate, personalize, and deliver professional certificates for training programs, seminars, church activities, and similar events. You choose a template, fill in recipient details, and we generate a PDF, email it to your recipients, and keep a record that lets anyone verify the certificate's authenticity later.

Your account and what we collect

To use Certsift, we collect your name, organization name, email address, and — if you provide it — a phone number, so we can identify your account and reach you if needed. We also keep a record of your token balance, payment history, and the batches and certificates you generate, since that's how the platform actually works. We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we only share what's necessary to process payments (via Paystack) and send emails (via our email provider).

Certificate files and how long we keep things

When you generate a certificate, we create a PDF and make it available for you to download. Your downloadable copy remains available for 21 days after generation, after which the file itself is removed from our storage — so please download and keep your own copy if you need it long-term; we don't guarantee to hold onto the actual PDF file forever. However, the verification record for every certificate we generate — the recipient's name, the program title, the issuing organization, the issue date, and the certificate number — is kept permanently on our system, so that anyone (an employer, a recipient, a third party) can always verify a certificate's authenticity through our public verification page, even years later.

Tokens and payments

Tokens are a prepaid credit used to generate certificates (1 certificate = 50 tokens). New accounts receive a free trial grant. Additional tokens are purchased through Paystack, and once purchased, are non-refundable except where a certificate genuinely fails to generate — in that specific case, the tokens for that failed certificate are automatically returned to your balance. We do not store your card details; all payment processing is handled directly by Paystack.

What we expect from you

You're responsible for the accuracy of the information you enter — recipient names, program titles, dates, and any images or signatures you upload. Certsift is a tool for generating legitimate certificates for real programs and achievements; you agree not to use it to create fraudulent, misleading, or impersonating documents. You're also responsible for keeping your account password secure and for anything done through your account.

No warranty, and a word on liability

We work hard to keep Certsift running reliably, but we can't promise it'll be perfect or available every second of every day — servers have bad days sometimes. Certsift isn't liable for indirect losses (like a missed event because an email arrived late), and our total liability to you in any situation is limited to the amount you've actually paid us in the past 2 months in tokens. This isn't us trying to dodge responsibility for real problems — it's just the standard, sensible boundary that lets a platform like this operate without insuring against every conceivable scenario.
