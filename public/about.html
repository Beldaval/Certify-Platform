About Certsift

Certsift was built for institutions of learning, churches, seminars, training programs, and anyone who regularly issues certificates and awards and wants to stop doing it one at a time in design software. Certsift lets you pick a template, fill in your recipients, organization, and program details, and Certsift handles the rest — generating each certificate, emailing it out, and keeping a permanent, publicly verifiable record so no one ever has to wonder if a certificate is real.

How to generate a certificate

  1. Choose a template. Certsift offers several pre-built certificate designs. Click through them and pick the one that fits your event or institution.
  2. Study the template before filling it in. Each template has different editable fields, shown as bolded — signature lines are editable or can be omitted completely if not needed, seals are optional, and some templates have extra fields. Take a moment to look at what's editable so you know exactly what you're filling in and how it'll appear.
  3. Fill in the details. Enter the recipient's name, the program or course title, and any other fields the template asks for. Upload a logo, signature image, and/or seal — accepted formats are PNG and JPEG.
  4. Generate. Click "Generate & send batch." Certsift creates the PDF, emails it to the recipient (if you provided their email), and shows you the status live with an instant download option.

Generating a batch of certificates

If you're issuing certificates to a group, click "Add another recipient" after your first entry. Certsift automatically carries forward shared details — like the program title, dates, and any uploaded logo or signatures — so you only have to re-enter what's actually different for each person (their name and email). You can add up to 50 recipients in a single batch.

"Copy me" — keeping your own record

Check the "Copy me" box before generating a batch, and Certsift will also send you a single email containing every certificate from that batch, in addition to whatever each recipient receives individually. This is the easiest way to keep a set for printing or your own records — remember, certificate files aren't stored on our system forever, so downloading or using "Copy me" is how you hold onto a permanent copy.

Verifying a certificate

Anyone — a recipient, an employer, a third party — can verify a certificate's authenticity on our homepage, with no login required. Just enter the certificate number exactly as it appears on the document, and Certsift will confirm the recipient, program, issuing organization, and issue date.
